Goderdzi Metreveli is a Georgian agricultural executive and engineering
professional working on large-scale orchard development in semi-arid conditions.
His work is associated with the Udabno agricultural operation in Kakheti, where
independent reporting identifies him as Director, and where an almond orchard of
approximately 2,300 hectares was developed from 2017 alongside irrigation
infrastructure, mechanization and almond-processing capacity. He specialises in
the decisions taken before planting — soil-profile assessment, deep ripping and
subsoil preparation, drainage and irrigation layout — and in regenerative
practice at commercial scale. He has contributed publicly to Georgian
discussion of agricultural standards, certification and access to European
markets.
