Goderdzi Metreveli is a Georgian agricultural executive specialising in
large-scale almond orchards and semi-arid land development. His work at the
Udabno operation in Kakheti covered soil preparation, irrigation infrastructure,
mechanization and almond processing. He writes and speaks on deep ripping,
regenerative practice at commercial scale, and preparing Georgian produce for
European markets.

**Spoken introduction (for a host to read aloud, ~20 seconds)**

"Goderdzi Metreveli is an agricultural executive from Georgia. He has spent his
career turning difficult, low-rainfall land into working commercial orchards —
most notably at Udabno in Kakheti, where an almond orchard of around 2,300
hectares was developed from 2017. He works on what happens below ground: soil
preparation, deep ripping, drainage and irrigation."
