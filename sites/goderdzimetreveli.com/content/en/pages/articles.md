Technical notes written from field practice in semi-arid Georgian conditions.

Each article separates two things explicitly: what I have observed directly, and
what is general guidance from published agronomic sources. The distinction is
marked visually in the text, because they carry different weight and a reader is
entitled to know which they are getting.

Where an article would benefit from project-specific figures I cannot yet
document, it says so rather than filling the space.
