## What to include

For consulting or technical enquiries, these details make a first reply much more
useful than a general exchange:

- **Location and climate** — district, annual rainfall, and whether irrigation
  water is available and secure
- **Land area** and the crop or orchard system being considered
- **Stage** — assessing land, planning establishment, mid-establishment, or
  improving an existing operation
- **Soil information** you already have — analyses, profile descriptions, or
  simply what a spade or an excavator has shown you
- **The specific question** you want answered

If you have dug a pit and photographed the face with a tape measure in it, that
one image will tell me more than several paragraphs of description.

## Types of enquiry

**Consulting.** Orchard establishment on difficult soils, soil-preparation
planning, deep-ripping specification, irrigation and mechanization strategy,
processing and value-chain assessment.

**Training.** Practical sessions for farm teams, machine operators and
agronomists on soil assessment, subsoil preparation, machinery selection and
orchard establishment quality.

**Speaking.** Conferences, industry events, agricultural education. See
[Speaking, Training and Consulting](/speaking/).

**Media.** Interviews, background briefings, technical fact-checking. See
[Media and Press](/media/).

## What I will not do

Stated plainly to save both sides time:

- Endorse specific equipment brands or suppliers commercially
- Provide a soil-preparation specification without seeing soil profile evidence
  from the actual site
- Confirm figures about past projects beyond what is documented on this site

## Privacy

Enquiry details are used only to respond to the enquiry. Nothing is added to a
mailing list, and this site uses privacy-respecting analytics with no advertising
or tracking cookies.
