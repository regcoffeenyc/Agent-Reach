## What will never be published here

Regardless of who requests it: signed contracts and commercial agreements;
employee personal data; personal address or identification documents; scanned
signatures, including on certificates; salary or shareholding information;
anything under a confidentiality obligation to a former employer; client or
supplier lists; land records containing third-party personal data.

Where a document is needed to *verify* a claim but must not be *published*, the
process is to review it privately and publish only the confirmed statement.

## Captioning standard

Every item added to this gallery carries: what it shows, where, when, who took
or issued it, and — for figures — the verification status used elsewhere on this
site. An uncaptioned photograph is not evidence of anything.
