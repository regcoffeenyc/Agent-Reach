summary that mixes verified and unverified entries without saying which is which
is worth less than one that does, particularly to anyone considering a
professional engagement.

## Areas of responsibility

Independent of title and date, the functional scope of the work is
straightforward to describe:

**Land development.** Assessment of undeveloped semi-arid land for horticultural
suitability; soil-profile survey; subsoil preparation planning; erosion
mitigation; earthworks and land forming; farm road and operational
infrastructure.

**Orchard establishment.** Block layout and row alignment; planting programme
sequencing against infrastructure readiness; establishment quality control
across multi-year planting phases; nursery and rootstock supply.

**Water infrastructure.** Water source development; storage; mainline and
distribution design; filtration and fertigation; irrigation scheduling under
constrained supply.

**Mechanization.** Fleet specification for heavy and compacted soils; matching
implement to soil condition; harvest machinery chains; workshop and maintenance
capability.

**Processing.** Primary processing — hulling, shelling, drying. Secondary
processing capability. Matching processing throughput to harvest logistics.

**Standards and market access.** GeoGAP certification; European market
requirements; traceability; organic-transition planning.

**Operational management.** Seasonal workforce planning at scale; contractor
management; capital project planning; supplier and partner relationships.
