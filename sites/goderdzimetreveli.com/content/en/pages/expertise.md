Five areas, each with a detailed page and a cluster of technical articles behind
it. They are not independent — the reason deep ripping matters is that it
determines what irrigation can achieve, and the reason processing capacity
matters is that it determines when the orchard can be harvested. The
interdependence is the point.

## [Regenerative Agriculture in Semi-Arid Georgia](/regenerative-agriculture/)

Restoring soil function on low-rainfall, low-organic-matter land while the
operation stays commercially viable. Erosion control, organic-transition
planning, nutrient management, irrigation efficiency, and how to measure
improvement in something other than yield.

The hard constraint is that a practice which cannot survive a bad season will not
be adopted, however sound the soil science behind it.

## [Almond Orchard Development](/almond-orchards/)

Planning and establishing commercial almond orchards: site assessment, variety
selection for continental and semi-arid conditions, block layout, planting
sequence, irrigation design, harvest planning and machinery requirements.

At scale, the binding problems are sequencing problems — keeping planting,
irrigation construction, harvest capacity and processing capacity in step with
each other.

## [Deep-Ripping and Soil Preparation](/deep-ripping/)

Identifying restrictive soil layers and deciding whether to open them; the
distinction between subsoiling and deep ripping; soil-moisture requirements;
depth, spacing and direction; the relationship to drainage and irrigation;
post-ripping settlement; and the cases where ripping should not be done at all.

The most consequential and least reversible decision in orchard establishment,
and the one most often made on assumption rather than evidence.

## [Irrigation and Agricultural Infrastructure](/irrigation-infrastructure/)

Engineering productive agricultural systems where water is the binding
constraint: source development, storage, distribution, filtration and
fertigation, scheduling under limited supply, drainage, and the roads and
buildings a large operation depends on.

Also: [Agricultural Mechanization](/mechanization/) — fleet specification for
heavy soils, matching implement to condition, and harvest machinery chains.

## [Almond Processing and Export Development](/almond-processing-export/)

Primary and secondary processing, matching throughput to harvest logistics,
quality standards for European markets, GeoGAP and commercial market access, and
regional processing capacity that independent growers can use.
