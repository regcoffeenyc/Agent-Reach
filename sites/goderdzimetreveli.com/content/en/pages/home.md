Goderdzi Metreveli is a Georgian agricultural executive and engineering
professional. His work has centred on the semi-arid Sagarejo district of
Kakheti, where independent reporting identifies him as Director of the Udabno
agricultural operation, and where an almond orchard of approximately 2,300
hectares was developed from 2017 alongside the irrigation infrastructure,
mechanization and processing capacity it required.

His technical focus is the part of orchard development that happens before a
tree is planted: reading the soil profile, finding the restrictive layers that
stop roots, opening them at the right moisture and depth, and laying out
drainage and irrigation so that the decisions made in year one do not become
constraints in year ten. Those decisions are largely irreversible, and they set
the ceiling on everything that follows.

He writes here about that work — what held up under field conditions, and what
did not.
