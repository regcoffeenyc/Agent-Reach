## Institutional publications

### Chemonics / USAID Zrda Activity — October 2021
**"A Local Certification System in Georgia: Steppingstones to Meeting Market
Demands — The GeoGAP Model"**, by Cynthia Steen and the Agriculture and Food
Security Practice Team.

Quotes Goderdzi Metreveli twice by name, attributed as **Managing Director,
Adjara Group**. Records that Adjara Group planned to earn GeoGAP certification at
its almond orchard, berry farm and vineyard. This is the strongest single piece of
independent documentation on this site: a USAID-funded technical publication
quoting him directly on the record.

[Read the brief (PDF)](https://www.chemonics.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/A-Local-Certification-System-in-Georgia_Steppingstones-to-Meeting-Market-Demands.pdf)

### UN Global Compact Network Georgia — 24 October 2023
**"Corporate Responsibility Award 2023 — Business for SDGs"**

Names **Udabno Group – Udabno Regenerative Transition** as the SME-category
winner under SDG 12. Ceremony held 12 October 2023 at the Rustaveli Theatre.

This confirms an **organisational** award. The same announcement lists individual
honourees separately under its SDG Pioneer category; Goderdzi Metreveli is not
among them, and this site does not present the award as personal.

[Read the announcement](https://unglobalcompact.ge/en/korporaciuli-mdgradobis-jildo-biznesi-mdgradi-ganvitarebistvis-gamarjvebulebi-cnobilia/)

### Adjara Group — 21 July 2021
**USAID and Adjara Group partnership announcement**

Confirms a Memorandum of Understanding signed by USAID/Georgia Mission Director
Peter Wiebler and directors of Adjara Group, covering hospitality, creative
industries and agriculture, with a geographic focus on Abastumani and
Samtskhe-Javakheti.

Goderdzi Metreveli is **not named** in this announcement.

[Read the announcement](https://adjaragroup.com/blog/6759652d7149498871569c63)

## Trade and business press

### EastFruit — 21 September 2021
**"Almonds harvesting in Georgia's largest orchard (video)"**

Identifies him as **director of Udabno**. Reports the orchard at 2,300 hectares
with 1,000 hectares harvested in 2021; varieties Guara, Supernova and Lauren;
calibre 29–30 mm; a harvest **forecast** of 500–600 tonnes; and processing
equipment with a capacity of 2.5 tonnes per hour. Notes the intention to offer
processing services to other Georgian farmers, and a planned transition to
certified organic production "in a few years".

[Read the article](https://east-fruit.com/en/news/almonds-harvesting-in-georgias-largest-orchard-video/)

### Investor.ge — 18 April 2022
**"The changing landscape of Georgian agriculture"**, by Sally White.
Republished by *Georgia Today*, 9 May 2022.

Identifies him as **Director of Udabno**. Reports GEL 150 million invested in the
operation; 2,300 hectares of almond orchard developed since 2017; more than
675,000 trees of Spanish and Italian origin; an 800-tonne in-shell crop in 2021;
approximately 150,000 nursery rootstocks; livestock of 74 buffalo, 285 cows and
1,500 sheep; and seasonal employment of more than 300 people.

[Read the article](https://www.investor.ge/2022/04/18/the-changing-landscape-of-georgian-agriculture/) ·
[Georgia Today version](https://georgiatoday.ge/the-changing-landscape-of-georgian-agriculture/)

### Caucasus Business Week
**"Turning Desert into an Agricultural Oasis: An Interview with Goderdzi
Metreveli"**

Interview describing him as **Director of Udabno Shelling**. Self-reported
figures include approximately 20,000 hectares of farmland, 2,000 hectares of
almonds, 150 permanent and 200–500 seasonal employees, and Europe and Gulf
countries as target markets.

As an interview, the figures here are self-reported rather than independently
verified, and the 20,000-hectare figure is not corroborated by any other
publication. That is noted on the [Selected Achievements](/achievements/) page.

[Read the interview](https://cbw.ge/business/turning-desert-into-an-agricultural-oasis-an-interview-with-goderdzi-metreveli)

## For journalists and editors

Available on request: a downloadable professional biography, a 100-word short
biography and a 50-word speaker biography, a professional portrait, and project
photography with captions and dates.

Interview requests, background briefings and technical fact-checking on soil
preparation, orchard establishment, irrigation or almond processing are welcome
in Georgian and English. [Contact](/contact/).

If you are fact-checking a figure attributed to me in an earlier publication,
please ask — several numbers in circulation come from interviews rather than
audited records, and I would rather correct them than see them repeated.
