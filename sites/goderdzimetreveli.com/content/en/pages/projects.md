Two case studies. Both are written to include the parts that went wrong, because
a case study that reports only successes teaches nothing and signals that the
author is selling rather than explaining.

## [Developing Commercial Agriculture in Georgia's Semi-Arid Udabno Region](/projects/udabno-semi-arid-development/)

Converting low-rainfall, erosion-prone land in Sagarejo into a working
agricultural operation from 2017 onward: soil assessment, subsoil preparation,
water infrastructure built where none existed, orchard establishment, erosion
control, roads and buildings, and mechanization for heavy dry ground.

Includes the published disagreement over the operation's total land area, and why
this site does not pick the largest figure.

## [Building a Large-Scale Almond Production and Processing Value Chain](/projects/almond-value-chain/)

From orchard to primary and secondary processing: variety selection for European
markets, harvest logistics, processing capacity, and the plan to make regional
processing available to independent Georgian almond and walnut growers.

Includes why the published processing-capacity figure and the figure in my own
record differ by roughly six times, and why neither is presented as settled.
