## Consulting

Engagements are practical rather than advisory-in-the-abstract. The most useful
work usually happens before planting, when decisions are still open.

**Site assessment before establishment.** Soil-profile survey, identification of
restrictive layers, drainage assessment, and an honest answer on whether the site
suits the crop being proposed. Sometimes that answer is no, and it is cheaper to
hear it at this stage than in year six.

**Soil-preparation specification.** Whether deep ripping is justified; if so, at
what depth, spacing, direction and soil moisture; what machinery is required; and
how the work should be sequenced against drainage and irrigation installation.

**Irrigation and infrastructure planning.** Source, storage, distribution,
filtration and fertigation; scheduling under constrained supply; and phasing
construction against planting.

**Mechanization strategy.** Fleet specification for heavy or compacted soils,
implement matching, harvest machinery chains, and maintenance capability.

**Processing and value-chain assessment.** Matching processing throughput to
harvest logistics; assessing whether regional processing is viable.

**Second opinion on an existing plan.** Frequently the highest-value engagement:
reviewing a specification someone else has produced, before capital is committed.

## Training

Delivered to farm teams, machine operators, agronomists and agricultural
students. Practical, in-field where possible.

- Reading a soil profile: digging, describing and interpreting a pit
- Identifying restrictive layers and deciding whether to treat them
- Assessing soil moisture for tillage decisions — by hand, in the field
- Deep-ripping operations: setup, depth control, direction, quality checking
- Machinery selection and implement matching for heavy soils
- Orchard establishment quality control
- Irrigation system operation and maintenance
- Erosion recognition and mitigation

The soil-pit session is the one that changes how people work. Most operators have
never seen the layer they have been working above.
