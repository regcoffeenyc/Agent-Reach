# SEO Titles and Meta Descriptions

Every page and article, with its title, meta description and primary topic.
These are the values in each content file's front matter and are what the theme
emits — there is no SEO plugin to override them.

**Conventions applied**

- Titles 50–60 characters where possible, so they are not truncated in results.
- Meta descriptions 140–160 characters.
- The name appears in the title only where it earns its place — the homepage,
  About, and the pillar pages that carry name+topic queries. Repeating it on
  every article title wastes characters that could describe the article.
- Every description is written to be *read*, not stuffed. It states what the page
  gives the reader.
- No page title duplicates another.

Character counts below are of the title as written.

## Not indexed

No titles or descriptions are needed for these — the theme sends
`noindex, follow` (see `inc/seo.php`, doc 01 §5):

category archives, tag archives, author archives, date archives, search results,
attachment pages (redirected), paginated pages beyond page 1 of `/articles/`, and
any non-production hostname.
