# 12-Month Editorial Calendar

**Cadence: two published items per month.** One technical article and one
secondary item (a Georgian translation, an evidence-page addition, or a review
pass on existing content).

Two per month is deliberately modest. On a personal-reputation domain, thin
content is actively harmful — it dilutes the topical signal and gives a reader
arriving from a name search a reason to doubt the rest. Ten genuinely expert
articles outperform fifty adequate ones, and the ten already published set the
standard the next twelve months have to match.

**Prerequisite:** no publishing during the first 30 days after launch (doc 06,
Phase 7). Month 1 below begins after that window closes.

## Standards every article must meet

Non-negotiable, from the brief:

- Author name and a link to the biography
- Publication date and last-reviewed date
- **First-hand observation visually separated from general guidance** (`.gm-firsthand`)
- Practical recommendations, not summary
- Original photography, diagrams or data where available
- References to reliable technical sources
- Links to the cluster pillar and 2–3 related pages
- One primary topic, assigned from doc 09, not already owned by another page
- No figure without a source or a verification label
- `[VERIFICATION REQUIRED]` on anything undocumented

**Do not publish** to hit a date. A month with one strong article beats a month
with one strong article and one thin one — the thin one costs more than it earns.

## What would justify changing this plan

- **A cluster starts ranking.** Add depth there rather than opening a new cluster.
- **Documentation arrives.** A resolved land-area or processing-capacity figure is
  worth an unscheduled update to the case studies and the achievements page —
  correcting the record is higher-value than a new article.
- **A query appears in Search Console you have not covered.** Real demand beats a
  planned topic.
- **Someone cites an article.** Look at what they used it for, and write the
  adjacent piece.
- **A source is corrected or retracted.** Update immediately; do not wait for a
  scheduled slot.
