## 1. How to read this report

Every factual claim proposed for the website was checked against independently
published sources retrieved during the build. Claims are graded:

| Grade | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Independently confirmed** | Two or more independent published sources agree, or one authoritative institutional source (USAID/Chemonics, UN Global Compact) states it directly. | Publish as written. |
| **B — Single-source / self-reported** | Appears in one publication, or is attributed to Goderdzi Metreveli himself in an interview. | Publish **only with in-text attribution** ("in a 2022 interview with *Caucasus Business Week*, Metreveli said…"). Never present as an independent fact. |
| **C — Contradicted** | Sources disagree materially. | **Hold.** Resolve with primary documentation before publishing any figure. |
| **D — No public source** | Supplied in the professional record only; nothing found in public sources. | **Hold** — mark `[VERIFICATION REQUIRED]` and publish only after a primary document is supplied. |
| **E — Do not publish** | Would misattribute an organisational achievement to an individual, or asserts something the source explicitly does not say. | **Remove.** |

**Governing principle applied throughout:** where the professional record supplied
by Goderdzi Metreveli conflicts with independently published reporting, the
website defers to the published source, or stays silent. It never publishes the
more flattering number.

### 3.2 Land area — **contradicted, highest-risk figure on the site**

| Figure | Source | Wording |
|---|---|---|
| ~20,000 ha farmland | **S1** | "20,000 hectares of farmland currently owned" — **self-reported in interview** |
| "over 5,000 ha of agricultural land" invested in since 2017 | S5 (via search index — **unverified**) | "invested in over 5,000 hectares of agricultural land" |
| "more than 4,000 hectares of land" | **S6** | "…more than 4,000 hectares of land and intends to develop the area into a farm project" |
| 5,000-ha *almond orchard* | **S9** | "A 5,000-hectare almond orchard has been successfully established in the village Udabno" — **conflicts with every later almond figure; likely reporting error** |

**Grade: C — Contradicted.** Published figures for the total landholding range from
4,000 to 20,000 hectares — a fivefold spread. The 20,000 ha figure appears **only**
in Metreveli's own interview and is not independently corroborated anywhere.

**Action:** **Hold the 20,000 ha figure.** Do not put it in a headline, a metric
tile, or structured data. Two acceptable options:

1. *Preferred:* omit a total-area figure entirely until land-registry or company
   records resolve it; describe scale qualitatively instead.
2. *If a figure is required:* attribute explicitly and disclose the range —
   "In a 2022 interview Metreveli described the operation as working
   approximately 20,000 hectares; other published accounts cite figures between
   4,000 and 5,000 hectares, and the distinction between land owned, leased and
   under active cultivation has not been publicly clarified."

The site as delivered uses option 1 on all metric displays and option 2 in the
body of the case study.

> **Likely underlying explanation to test against documents:** the figures may
> describe different things — total licensed/leased territory vs. land acquired
> vs. land under cultivation. Resolving this cleanly is the single highest-value
> verification task on this list.
